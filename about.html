About & Method — FMU Past Papers Quiz

The FMU Past Papers Quiz is a free MCQ practice tool for 1st Year MBBS students of Faisalabad Medical University (FMU), affiliated with the University of Health Sciences (UHS), Lahore.

Who made it

Habib Ullah, a 1st Year MBBS student at Faisalabad Medical University. The quiz was built by a student, for students.

What it covers

2,771 questions: 2,591 past-paper-topic MCQs (Anatomy 625, Physiology 1,156 including Guyton-based questions, Biochemistry 626, plus 184 from Histology, Embryology, Pathology and other subjects) plus 180 extra practice questions. Every question carries an explanation referenced to standard textbooks: Guyton and Hall, Lippincott, Harper, and BD Chaurasia.

How questions are sourced

Questions are built from past-paper topics and patterns (FMU send-ups, professional exams, and assessments) plus original practice questions in the same style. They are a practice tool, not a verbatim reproduction of official UHS papers.

How answers are checked

Each explanation is referenced to a named standard textbook. If you find an error, report it via Instagram (@habib.ullah141) — corrections are logged publicly on the Corrections Log.

What this site is not

This site is not affiliated with UHS or FMU, and it is not a substitute for textbooks or lectures. It is an exam-preparation practice tool only, not medical advice.
