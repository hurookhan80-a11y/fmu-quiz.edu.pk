Home › Anatomy MCQs › Upper Limb

The upper limb consists of the shoulder girdle, arm, forearm, and hand. Its bones are the clavicle, scapula, humerus, radius, and ulna, together with the bones of the wrist and hand. The brachial plexus supplies its muscles, while the axillary, brachial, radial, and ulnar arteries carry its blood supply.

  1. Damage to which muscle causes loss of flexion at distal interphalangeal joint?

    • A. Dorsal interosseous
    • B. Palmer interosseous
    • C. Lumbrical
    • D. Flexor digitorum profundus
    • E. Flexor digitorum superficialis
    Show answer and explanation

    Answer: D. Flexor digitorum profundus

    Flexor digitorum profundus inserts on the base of the distal phalanx and is the only muscle that flexes the distal interphalangeal joint. Flexor digitorum superficialis stops at the middle phalanx and flexes the PIP joint, while interossei and lumbricals mainly flex the MCP joints and extend the IP joints.
