Thorax MCQs for FMU 1st Year MBBS Anatomy

Home › Anatomy MCQs › Thorax

The thorax is the upper part of the trunk, enclosed by the thoracic cage formed by the ribs, sternum, and thoracic vertebrae. It contains the lungs and pleura, the heart and great vessels, the oesophagus, and the mediastinum. The diaphragm forms its floor and is the chief muscle of breathing.

  1. A tumour in the posterior mediastinum compresses which structure:

    • A. Esophagus
    • B. Arch of aorta
    • C. IVC
    • D. Pulmonary trunk
    • E. Trachea
    Show answer and explanation

    Answer: A. Esophagus

    The esophagus descends through the posterior mediastinum behind the trachea and heart, so a posterior mediastinal tumor compresses it and causes dysphagia. The arch of aorta and pulmonary trunk are in the middle/superior mediastinum, and the trachea is mainly in the superior mediastinum.

    Source: KLM Clinically Oriented Anatomy — Ch. 3: Thorax

  2. If tumour is in breast it most likely to teach vertebral column by

    • A. Lymph vessel
    • B. Cephalic vein
    • C. Intercostal veins
    • D. Lateral thoracic vein
    Show answer and explanation

    Answer: C. Intercostal veins

    Breast cancer cells can reach the vertebral column through intercostal veins that communicate with the valveless vertebral venous plexus of Batson. This allows retrograde spread to vertebrae bypassing lungs. Lymphatics go first to axillary nodes, and the cephalic/lateral thoracic veins drain to the axillary vein, not directly to vertebrae.

    Source: KLM Clinically Oriented Anatomy — Ch. 3: Thorax

  3. A man after a road traffic accident presented to the emergency with fracture of the medial side of clavicle and posterior dislocation of the sternoclavicular joint. Which structure will most likely be damaged?

  48. Bronchial arteries (systemic blood supply to lungs) arise mainly from:

    Bronchial arteries are systemic vessels from the descending thoracic aorta (left) and posterior intercostal arteries (right) supplying bronchial walls and lung connective tissue. Pulmonary arteries carry deoxygenated blood for gas exchange, a separate circulation.
