Privacy

The FMU Past Papers Quiz does not require an account, does not show ads, and does not use analytics or tracking scripts.

What is stored

Your bookmarks, mistakes, quiz statistics, and theme choice are stored only in your own browser (localStorage) on your own device, so they survive a page refresh. Nothing is sent to any server. You can clear this data at any time from your browser settings; the quiz keeps working without it.

What is not collected

No names, email addresses, passwords, or payment details are collected. The optional "Send a Review" box only opens your own email or Instagram app to send a message you write yourself.

Links

The site links to Instagram (@habib.ullah141) and to textbook sources. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent via Instagram (@habib.ullah141).
